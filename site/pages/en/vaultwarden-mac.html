What is Verrou?

Verrou is a free, open source password manager app for macOS and iPhone that works with your own Vaultwarden server. It reads and writes your vault in the standard Bitwarden format, so the official Bitwarden apps and the web vault keep working next to it. Nothing to migrate, nothing to export.

Why a native Vaultwarden client on Mac

  • Opens in a blink, finds as you type. Fuzzy, accent-insensitive search, under 5 ms on 10,000 items, with filters like type:card.
  • Quick Access from any app. ⌘ ⇧ Space, type, then copy a password or a one-time code without opening the main window.
  • Fills any Mac app. ⌘ \ types the username and password into the app in front of you, browser or not.
  • Touch ID, a YubiKey or your master password. The vault key is sealed by the Secure Enclave; the vault locks after inactivity, on sleep and with the screen.
  • Works offline. Edits, new items, trash and archive are kept and synced in order as soon as the server answers.

Everything your Vaultwarden vault holds

Logins with one-time codes (TOTP), passkeys, cards, identities, secure notes, SSH keys, attachments, Wi-Fi networks with a QR code to join, and custom fields. Shared vaults too: organizations, collections, groups and members, confirmed by fingerprint phrase, plus Send and emergency access.

Browser extension, terminal and SSH agent

The Verrou extension fills logins, one-time codes, cards and passkeys in Brave, Chrome and Firefox, only for the site in front of you, over an encrypted local channel. From the terminal, verrou searches without ever seeing a secret, signs with the SSH keys in your vault, and hands secrets to commands without printing them: see secrets for AI agents.

Install

  1. Download Verrou-{{version}}-macos.dmg, open it and drag Verrou to Applications. It is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
  2. Open Verrou, enter your Vaultwarden address, e-mail and master password, then your second factor. Touch ID unlocks it from then on.
  3. Optional: in Settings, link the browser extension and the verrou command.

Verrou updates itself: it checks once a day, downloads in the background and installs when you quit, after checking the update's signature.

Private by design

Your vault is decrypted only on your Mac, by Bitwarden's official Rust SDK. Verrou talks to your server; the only other connections (breach checks with 5 characters of a hash, update checks) are listed in the privacy policy. No account of ours, no analytics.
