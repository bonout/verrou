Support

Help with Verrou.

Get help

  • E-mail: contact@bonout.com, for any question about Verrou.
  • Bugs and ideas: open an issue on GitHub.
  • Security: report a vulnerability as described in SECURITY.md, not in a public issue.

Before writing, the FAQ and the guide may already have your answer.

What to include in a bug report

  • The version of Verrou and of macOS or iOS.
  • The steps that lead to the problem, and what you expected to happen.
  • On Mac and iPhone, the crash and hang reports from Settings → Diagnostics. They stay on your device until you share them.

Quick answers

How do I set up AutoFill on iPhone?

Open Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords and turn on Verrou in the list of apps. Then tap a login field: Verrou offers the site's accounts above the keyboard.

How do I set up AutoFill on Mac?

Open System Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords and turn on Verrou. Safari and other Mac apps are then filled through the system's AutoFill.

How do I turn on the Safari extension on iPhone?

Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Verrou, turn it on and allow it on all websites. In Safari, Verrou can then submit the sign-in form once AutoFill has filled it.
