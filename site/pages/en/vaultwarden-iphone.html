Vaultwarden for iPhone

Your Vaultwarden vault, native on iPhone.

Face ID, AutoFill in Safari and apps, one-time codes and passkeys: the same vault as on your Mac, in an app made for iOS.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0) · iOS 17 or later · Liquid Glass on iOS 26 · coming to the App Store

What is Verrou for iPhone?

Verrou is a free, open source iPhone app for Vaultwarden. It signs in to your own server (with a TOTP or e-mail second factor), keeps the vault in the standard Bitwarden format, and unlocks with Face ID. The official Bitwarden apps keep working next to it.

AutoFill everywhere

  • Passwords in Safari and apps, from the keyboard's suggestion bar, after Face ID.
  • One-time codes suggested on the code field, and copied for you.
  • Passkeys: create and use them, stored in your vault in the Bitwarden format.
  • Set up codes in Verrou: scan a two-factor QR code (camera, screenshot, clipboard or Google Authenticator export), or set Verrou as the app that opens every setup code link.

Everything on the go

Search as you type, tap to copy (a local-only clipboard that iOS clears after 90 seconds), cards, identities, Wi-Fi QR codes, attachments, a password and passphrase generator, an audit, offline edits, and shared vaults with your family or team: share items, manage organizations, collections and members, and send a text or a file with Send.

Approve your Mac's requests

When the terminal or an AI agent on your Mac needs a secret while you're away, the request comes to Verrou on your iPhone, with the reason given. Face ID approves it; the vault key never leaves the Mac. Read how it works in secrets for AI agents.

Private by design

The vault is decrypted only on your iPhone. Verrou locks in the background, hides its content in the app switcher, and talks only to your server (and, if you pair your Mac, to your own iCloud account, sealed end to end).
