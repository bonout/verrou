Terminal & AI agents

Secrets for your commands, not for your scrollback.

verrou run hands secrets to a command in its environment. They never land in the terminal, its history, or an AI agent's context, and anything the command prints is masked.

Plus an SSH agent that signs with the keys in your vault, and git commit signing. Every secret asks for your approval.

# .env: commit it, there's no secret in it
GITHUB_TOKEN=verrou://GitHub/token
DATABASE_URL=verrou://Production%20DB/url

$ verrou --reason "deploy" run --env-file .env -- ./deploy.sh
Touch ID · or your iPhone if you're away
Deploying with token <concealed by Verrou>
✓ done

$ eval "$(verrou ssh-agent)" && git commit -S

Private by design

Nothing to trust but your own server.

No account of ours, no analytics, no tracking. Every connection is documented, with its known limits.

  • Bitwarden's cryptography, end to end: the vault is decrypted only on your devices.
  • Secure Enclave keys for Touch ID, Face ID and iPhone approvals.
  • The extension only receives the logins of the site in front of it, over an encrypted local channel.
  • Copied secrets are hidden from clipboard managers and cleared after 90 seconds.
  • The terminal searches without ever seeing a secret; each one asks for your approval.
  • Open source, GPL-3.0, with a build attestation for every release file.
