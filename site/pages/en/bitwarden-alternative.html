Verrou or the Bitwarden app?

Same vault, same format, same server. Verrou is a native app for people who self-host with Vaultwarden and live on a Mac and an iPhone.

The short answer

Verrou is not a fork or a replacement of Bitwarden: it is another client for the same vault. It works with your Vaultwarden server in the standard Bitwarden format, so you can keep the official Bitwarden apps, browser extension and web vault, and use Verrou where you prefer it. Stop using Verrou any time: nothing changes on your server.

When Verrou is the better fit

  • You run Vaultwarden at home or for your team, and use a Mac and an iPhone.
  • You want a vault that opens instantly and finds as you type, from any app.
  • You work in the terminal or with AI agents and want secrets approved one by one, from your iPhone when you're away.

When to stay with the official apps

  • You use bitwarden.com: Verrou is built and tested against Vaultwarden only.
  • You need Windows, Linux desktop or Android apps.
  • You rely on Bitwarden's business features (SSO, admin console, Secrets Manager).

Switching is reversible

Everything stays on your server in the Bitwarden format, including passkeys, attachments and shared vaults. Install Verrou next to the Bitwarden app, sign in to the same server, and compare.
