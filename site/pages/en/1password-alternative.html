The 1Password experience, on your own server.

Verrou brings what people love in 1Password on the Mac (Quick Access, filling any app, an SSH agent, secrets for the terminal) to a vault you host yourself with Vaultwarden. Free and open source.

The short answer

Choose Verrou if you want your passwords on a server you control, with a native Mac and iPhone app. Choose 1Password if you want a hosted service that works on every platform, with nothing to run yourself.

Moving from 1Password

Export your vault from 1Password (.1pux), import it in Verrou, then clean up duplicates: the same account under different URLs, titles or usernames is found and merged into one item. Your items land on your Vaultwarden server, where the official Bitwarden apps can read them too.

What you give up

  • Running a server. Vaultwarden is light (it runs on a Raspberry Pi), but it's yours to update and back up.
  • Windows and Android apps. Verrou is for the Mac and the iPhone; the official Bitwarden apps cover the rest with the same vault.
  • A company behind it. Verrou is a young open source project: read SECURITY.md, which documents every connection and its known limits.
