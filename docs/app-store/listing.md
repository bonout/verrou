**Description** (1544/4000)

```
Verrou is a fast, native password manager for your own Vaultwarden server. It works with the vault you already have: the official Bitwarden apps and web vault keep working next to it.

YOUR SERVER, YOUR DATA
• Connects only to the Vaultwarden server you choose
• End-to-end encryption with Bitwarden's official crypto: your vault is encrypted on the iPhone before anything is sent
• No account with us, no analytics, no ads, no tracking
• Open source: every line can be audited on GitHub

UNLOCK IN A GLANCE
• Face ID unlock, with your master password as a fallback
• Auto-lock in the background and a privacy screen in the app switcher
• Protected items ask for Face ID again before showing their secrets

FILL EVERYWHERE
• AutoFill for passwords, one-time codes and passkeys in Safari and your apps
• Save new passkeys straight into your vault

FIND ANYTHING
• Instant search across thousands of items, as you type, typos forgiven
• Logins, cards, identities, secure notes, Wi-Fi networks, SSH keys and more
• Folders, favorites, shared vaults and organizations

STAY SECURE
• Built-in two-factor codes (TOTP), scanned from a QR code
• Password and passphrase generator
• Security audit: weak, reused and old passwords
• Breach checks with Have I Been Pwned's k-anonymity: your passwords never leave the iPhone

WORKS OFFLINE
• Your vault stays available without a network; changes sync when you're back online

Verrou needs a Vaultwarden (or Bitwarden-compatible) server. It is not affiliated with Bitwarden, Inc. or the Vaultwarden project.
```
